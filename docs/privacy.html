Mixtape Pro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026 (rev. 2)

In short: Mixtape Pro is a music player. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. Your music, playlists and settings stay on your phone.

Who we are

Mixtape Pro ("the app") is published on Google Play by AbhiYadav (an individual developer). Contact: abhishek6@gmail.com.

What the app accesses on your phone, and why

  • Music and audio files (audio / storage permission): to find and play the songs already on your phone, read their tags and cover art, and, only when you ask, delete a track or export a mix. These files are never uploaded by the app.
  • Microphone (record audio permission), used for two things:
    • The spectrum visualizer: Android requires this permission for the app to analyse the sound it is playing. Nothing is recorded or kept for this.
    • Voice-overs in DJ Studio's Mashup Studio: only when you tap record, your voice is recorded and saved as an audio file on your phone so you can layer it into a mix. The recording stays on your device and is never uploaded by the app.
    You can refuse the permission; the visualizer then uses a simulated animation and voice-over recording is unavailable.
  • Notifications: to show the playback controls while music plays in the background.
  • System settings (modify system settings permission): only if you choose to set a song as your ringtone.

Your library, mixtapes, favourites, settings and any crash log are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

Internet features and the services they contact

Some features connect to third-party services. When they do, your phone's IP address and the request (for example a song title) reach that service, as with any internet request. The app sends no account, contact, location or device identifiers.

  • Internet radio: the stream of the station you choose (for example SomaFM, 181.fm, NTS, KEXP and others). The app also briefly tests stations at launch to show which are on air.
  • Artwork and track details: the song title and artist are looked up on the iTunes Search API (Apple) and Deezer to find missing album art and information.
  • Lyrics: the song title and artist are looked up on LRCLIB.
  • Online music: searches you type in the Streaming tab are sent to Audius and the Internet Archive.
  • Cloud links: if you paste a Google Drive, Dropbox or other link, the app downloads that file from it. The app does not sign in to these services.
  • Images: some station and demo pictures load from Unsplash and Pixabay.

These services have their own privacy policies. The app does not send them anything beyond what each request needs.

Purchases

Mixtape Pro is sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a crash log is saved on your phone. It is only sent if you choose to share it yourself (for example by email) from the dialog the app shows.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke any permission in Android settings; related features will be limited.
  • You can clear the app's data or uninstall it at any time to delete everything it stored.
  • Questions or requests: abhishek6@gmail.com.
